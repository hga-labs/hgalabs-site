Frequently asked questions

How do I get help with an app?

Email support@hgalabs.com or call or text (267) 291-4723. Texting gets the fastest reply. We usually respond within one business day.

How do I delete my account or my data?

If the app has a delete option in its settings, you can use it to delete your account and associated data. You can also email us to request deletion. See Data deletion requests below.

When will your apps be available?

Our apps are not released yet. LingoBloom, SI Skinaware and Soccer Spots are all coming soon. Soccer Spots is planned for iPhone and the web. Check our Apps section for updates.

Do you sell my data?

No. We do not sell personal data. Read our Privacy Policy for details.

I’m a parent. How do I ask about my child’s information?

Contact us at support@hgalabs.com and we will help review or delete your child’s information.

Data deletion requests

You can ask us to delete your account and the personal data associated with it at any time:

  1. Email support@hgalabs.com with the subject line “Data deletion request.”
  2. Include the name of the app and the email address or sign-in method linked to your account.
  3. We may ask you to confirm the request from that email address so that we can verify it is you.
  4. We will confirm when your data has been deleted, usually within 30 days.

Some information may be kept for a limited time where the law requires it or to prevent fraud or abuse, as described in our Privacy Policy.
