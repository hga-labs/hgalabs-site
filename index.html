Soccer Spots

Soccer Spots teaches young players where to stand, because every player has a spot. Kids drag their teammates’ dots onto the field, then watch the play run to see why each spot matters, with a friendly coach narrating. There are bite-size Practice Plays, a Live Match, Free Play and mini-games, plus a Coach’s Corner of drills for grown-ups who run practice.
