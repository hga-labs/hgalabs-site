Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HGA Labs LLC (“HGA Labs,” “we,” “us” or “our”) collects, uses and shares information when you use our mobile apps, web apps and this website at hgalabs.com (together, the “Services”). By using the Services, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

The information we collect depends on which app or feature you use. Not every app collects every type of information below.

  • Account information. If you create an account, we collect details such as your name or display name, email address and sign-in details (for example, a sign-in provided by Apple or Google).
  • Content you submit. Messages, voice or text input, answers, preferences, settings and other content you enter into an app, such as conversations in a language-practice app.
  • Camera images. Some features, such as AI skin analysis, use your phone’s camera. Photos you choose to take or upload are processed to provide that feature (for example, to generate your analysis and a personalized routine). We access the camera only with your permission, and only when you use a feature that needs it.
  • Usage and diagnostic data. Information about how the Services are used and how they perform, such as features used, app version, device type, operating system, crash reports and performance logs.
  • Communications. If you email, call or text us, we keep the information you share so we can respond.

This website does not require an account. Our hosting provider may automatically log basic technical information, such as IP address and browser type, when you visit.

2. How we use information

  • To provide, operate and maintain the Services and their features.
  • To personalize your experience, such as tailoring a practice conversation or a skincare routine.
  • To fix bugs, monitor performance and improve the Services.
  • To respond to support requests and communicate with you about the Services.
  • To keep the Services safe, prevent misuse and comply with legal obligations.

3. Third-party AI and cloud providers

We use trusted service providers to run the Services. For example, we may use Google Firebase for sign-in, data storage, hosting and analytics, and Google’s Gemini models to power AI features such as conversations and image analysis. When you use an AI feature, the content needed for that feature (such as your text, audio or a photo) is sent to the provider to generate a response. These providers process information on our behalf and under their own privacy and security terms. Our website is hosted by GitHub Pages.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share information only:

  • With service providers that help us operate the Services, as described above.
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users, HGA Labs or others.
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case this policy will continue to apply to your information.
  • With your consent or at your direction.

5. Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed to provide the Services and for the purposes described in this policy. Account information and content are kept while your account is active. When you delete your account or ask us to delete your data, we delete it or de-identify it within a reasonable time, unless we need to keep some information to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes or prevent abuse. Diagnostic logs are kept for a limited period.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Access and deletion. You can ask for a copy of your personal information, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing support@hgalabs.com. Where an app offers in-app account deletion, you can also use that option. See our Support page for instructions.
  • Camera and permissions. You can turn off camera, microphone or other permissions at any time in your device settings. Some features will not work without them.
  • Communications. You can opt out of non-essential emails by using the unsubscribe link or contacting us.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law. We will respond to verified requests as required by applicable law.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, such as encryption in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children’s privacy

Some of our apps, such as Soccer Spots, are designed for young players to use with a parent, guardian or coach. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent, as required by law. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has given us personal information, please contact us at support@hgalabs.com and we will review and delete it as appropriate.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date above and, if the changes are significant, provide additional notice, such as in the app or on this website.
